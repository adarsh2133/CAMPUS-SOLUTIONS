**🎓 STUNET**

STUNET is a student-centric side project built to help students connect, collaborate, and compete together. The platform enables students to find teammates for competitions, hackathons, and collaborative projects while fostering a strong and supportive student community.

**🚀 About the Project**

STUNET aims to solve a common problem faced by students — finding the right partners for hackathons, competitions, and academic or skill-based projects. Many students have ideas and skills but lack a reliable network to collaborate with. STUNET bridges this gap by providing a centralized platform where students can discover like-minded peers and grow together.

This project is designed as a community-driven platform, encouraging collaboration, learning, and long-term connections among students.

**✨ Key Features**

🔍 Find a Partner:
Connect with students based on skills, interests, and competition requirements.

👥 Student Community:
A space for discussions, collaboration, and networking among students.

🏆 Hackathon & Competition Pairing:
Easily form or join teams for hackathons, contests, and events.

📢 Announcements & Updates:
Stay updated with upcoming competitions and community activities.

🔐 User-Friendly Interface:
Simple, intuitive, and student-focused design.

(More features will be added as the platform evolves.)
